// ethics pledge

The learner's code

Everyone who learns here agrees to these five rules. They are the same rules working professionals follow.

  • I will only test systems I own or have written permission to test.
  • I will follow the laws of my country and the rules of every platform I use.
  • I will report weaknesses I find responsibly and privately.
  • I will protect the privacy of other people and their data.
  • I will share what I learn and help others grow.
