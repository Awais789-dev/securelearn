Frequently asked questions

Is SecureLearn really free?

Yes. All courses, cheat sheets and tools are free to use.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. Start with Cybersecurity Fundamentals, then follow the roadmap. Basic computer skills are enough.

Is it legal to practice hacking?

It is legal when you test systems you own or have written permission to test. Use your own lab or platforms made for practice. Never test anyone else's systems without permission.

Do the tools send my data anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded. Still, avoid typing real passwords into any website.

Do I get a certificate?

Not yet. The roadmap lists industry certifications that employers recognize.
